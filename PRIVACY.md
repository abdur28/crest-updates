# Crest — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-05_

> **Note:** This is a starting template for a private build shared among friends. Review it and adjust anything that doesn't match how you actually use the app before relying on it.

Crest is a macOS menu-bar / notch utility that runs entirely on your Mac. There is no Crest account, no Crest server, and no analytics or tracking.

## What stays on your device
Your settings, clipboard history, notes, timers, and similar data are stored locally on your Mac (in app preferences and local storage). Credentials for music services (e.g. Apple Music / Spotify tokens) are stored in the macOS Keychain. None of this is transmitted to the developer.

## When Crest uses the network
Crest only makes network requests for features you use:
- **Software updates** — fetches the update feed and release from this GitHub repository.
- **Weather** — queries a public weather API for the lock-screen widget (only if enabled).
- **Music artwork / controls** — talks to Apple Music / Spotify to show artwork and control playback.
- **AI assistant (optional)** — if you enable it, the prompts and screenshots you submit are sent to the AI provider you configure. Those are governed by that provider's privacy policy.

## System permissions Crest may request
Screen recording, microphone, camera, calendars, reminders, Bluetooth, and location — each is used only for its corresponding in-app feature and only after you grant it. You can revoke any of these in **System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security**.

## Sharing
Crest does not sell or share your data. Any third-party services listed above process data under their own privacy policies.
